# The Feeder
1. Add bearing to the feeder stepper mount
1. Add M3x8s and nuts
1. Mount 2GT on stepper
1. Loosly mount stepper to feeder stepper mount
1. Slide mount on end of extrusion, flush with end, do not tighten
1. Add 80T to flange
1. 80T/Flange/Washer on D Shaft
1. Gears and bearings on D Shaft
1. Knob/Belt on D Shaft
1. Slide stepper up and tighten
1. Add feet
1. Slide on 12mm (15mm?) spacer
1. Screws and nuts on feeder blocks
1. Feeder block on extrusion, align to first gear
1. Move gears to spaces, make sure spin freely
1. Tighten blocks to extrusion
1. Use filament to align geers
